What's New

  • A new Tip update channel in Settings → Updates follows every commit that passes CI, so you can run what main does without building it yourself. (#310)
  • The "Match terminal app backgrounds" toggle is now called Adaptive background and sits in Settings → Appearance → Window alongside the other background controls. (#311)

Fixes

  • Adaptive background now works when ghostty's background-opacity-cells is enabled — it previously did nothing at all in that case. (#309)
  • A pane matched to a terminal app's background now composites at the same opacity as the rest of the window, instead of reading noticeably more opaque than the app around it. (#309)

Notes

  • Tip builds are cut from every passing commit and are not release-tested. Stable remains the default channel, and Homebrew always tracks stable.

Thanks to @thdxg for this release.
